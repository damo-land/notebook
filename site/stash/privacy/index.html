Privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026.

Your notes stay yours

stash keeps your notes as plain markdown files in a folder you choose, for example in iCloud Drive or on your iPhone. The app only reads and writes that folder. Nothing is uploaded to us or to anyone else. If the folder is in iCloud Drive, Apple syncs it under your Apple Account.

We collect nothing

The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We cannot see your notes. There are no servers to see them with.

On-device chat

The optional chat uses Apple Intelligence on your iPhone when your device supports it. Your questions and notes are processed on the phone and never sent to a server.

Home screen widget

The widget shows your open tasks. The app keeps a small copy of those task titles on your device so the widget can read them. It never leaves your iPhone.

Deleting your data

Your notes are files in your folder. Delete them there, or delete the folder. Deleting the app removes its settings and the widget copy.
